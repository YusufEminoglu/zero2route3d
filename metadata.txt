about=02Route 3D is a unified multi-criteria 3D mobility and pathfinding studio for QGIS. It computes optimal 3D routes across complex terrain and urban networks with realistic slope, aspect, surface temperature (LST), tree shade, and road hierarchy resistances. Features include 9 specialised mobility profiles (Standard Adult, Senior, Child, Stroller, Wheelchair, Bicycle, E-Scooter, Passenger Car, Heavy Freight), Tobler and Minetti biomechanical energy and time equations, and a hardware-accelerated embedded 3D WebGL viewport with cinematic drone and chase camera animation, elevation profile HUD, and 3D LineStringZ export.
