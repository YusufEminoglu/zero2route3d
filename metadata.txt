about=02Route 3D is a unified multi-criteria 3D mobility and pathfinding studio for QGIS. It computes optimal 3D routes across real QGIS line layers or successfully downloaded OpenStreetMap networks with slope, aspect, surface temperature (LST), tree shade, and road hierarchy resistances. Missing network or elevation data is reported explicitly; no fabricated terrain or road network is used. Features include specialised mobility profiles, Tobler and Minetti biomechanical energy and time equations, an embedded 3D WebGL viewport, elevation profile HUD, and 3D LineStringZ export.
