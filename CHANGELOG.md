## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-22

### Added
- **Interactive QGIS Dock Studio:** Modern card-based UI with dark/light adaptive styling and point picker map tools.
- **Universal 3D Mobility Profiles:** 9 distinct mobility models (Standard Adult, Senior, Child, Stroller, Wheelchair, Bicycle, E-Scooter, Passenger Car, Heavy Freight).
- **Multi-Criteria 3D Kinematics:** Tobler hiking function, Minetti biomechanical metabolic equations, grade penalties, and vehicle road hierarchy models.
- **Environmental Cost Surfaces:** Dynamic sampling of DEM, slope, aspect, Land Surface Temperature (LST), and green shade impedance.
- **Embedded Hardware-Accelerated 3D WebGL Viewport:** 60 FPS Three.js canvas docked inside QGIS, with glowing 3D ribbons, drone/chase camera tracking, and interactive elevation profile HUD.
- **Vector & Data Export:** Native QGIS 3D vector layer (`LineStringZ`), GPX, GeoJSON, and standalone interactive 3D HTML export.
- **Processing Provider:** Native QGIS Processing algorithms for automated 3D routing and isochrone generation.
